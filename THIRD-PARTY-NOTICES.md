## Microsoft Power BI Visuals sample report

**Where it's used:**

- `reference/power-bi-visuals/`: a modified copy of the report
- `skills/power-bi-visuals/references/visual-catalog/`: JSON examples copied from it
- `pbi-starter/templates/demo/`: the visual gallery pages, whose visual JSON follows those examples, and the US states shape map file (`StaticResources/SharedResources/Shapemaps/usa.states.topo.json`) copied from it

**Source:** [microsoft/powerbi-desktop-samples](https://github.com/microsoft/powerbi-desktop-samples/tree/main/Power%20BI%20Visuals%20report)

**What changed:** all custom visuals and the page that held only them were removed, and the Power BI logo images were
replaced with a plain placeholder. Power BI and its logo are trademarks of Microsoft, and this license doesn't grant
rights to them.
